# Design Taste Evaluation Rubrics

## Design goal

Crypseal should feel like a precision tool, not a shell log dumped into a chat app.

## Score every screen 1–5

### Hierarchy
Primary action is obvious. Raw details do not dominate.

### Density
Enough information for power users without overwhelming a phone screen.

### Reversibility
Users can see what changed and undo it.

### State clarity
Running, waiting, failed, succeeded, needs approval, and blocked states are visually distinct.

### Trust clarity
Risk level and scope are visible before action.

### Thumb ergonomics
Primary actions are reachable and separated from destructive actions.

### Accessibility
Readable type, contrast, labels, dynamic type, touch targets.

## Required design reviews

- Approval card review
- Diff viewer review
- Command output review
- Onboarding review
- Project/thread switcher review
- Error/recovery flow review
- Long-running process monitor review

## Visual direction

Default to quiet professional: dark/light theme, Material 3-compatible, code/log surfaces with strong spacing, soft hierarchy, and no gimmicky AI-avatar focus.

The product personality should be: capable, calm, technical, human.
